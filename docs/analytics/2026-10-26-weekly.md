## Takeaways

- **The core of the game is watching people.** Selecting and following people is the most common action, Civilizations and Stats are the most-used panels, and Family tree, Lineages, Scenarios and Achievements are barely opened.
- **The star prompt mostly gets dismissed.**
- **Retention is the open question.** Daily users have fallen since launch, but users who do stay tend to stay a long time.

## Caveats

- "Clicks" only means the events the game reports itself. PostHog's automatic click tracking is off, so anything without its own event (buttons, speed controls and so on) isn't counted.
- One user's panel opens arrived with Chinese names (文明, 编年史, 统计), apparently from some other build; the game itself always logs English. They're merged into Civilizations, Chronicles and Stats above.
